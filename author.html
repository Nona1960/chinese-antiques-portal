Research

Professor Nona Dronova works at the intersection of art history, materials science and the technology of the traditional applied arts. Her research focuses on the instrumental and visual-structural diagnostics of works of decorative art, above all ancient Chinese porcelain, jade and bronze.

Her method moves step by step from the macroscopic level — proportions, silhouette, composition — to the microscopic and physico-chemical parameters of the surface: glaze structure, crackle, inclusions, firing traces and the signatures of natural ageing. Porcelain is thus read not only as a work of art, but as a technological document of its time.
