Porcelain as a historical document

The character and forms of porcelain reflect the life and development of society, its ideas, culture and daily life in a given epoch. Every object carries the evidence of its own manufacture: the composition of its body and glaze, the method of forming and firing, the style of its decoration and the traces of natural ageing.

The research presented in this section approaches attribution as a sequence: examination of the object, identification of its material, dating of the period of manufacture, analysis of the decoration and marks, assessment of condition and, finally, appraisal.

Monograph

What You Need to Know About Antique Chinese Porcelain

A reference encyclopaedia that provides the information needed to attribute antique Chinese porcelain in accordance with international standards. Separate chapters are devoted to the appraisal and diagnosis of export, imperial and monochrome porcelain; the book examines the development of porcelain styles and the marks found on antique Chinese porcelain, and gives practical recommendations for detecting forgeries.
