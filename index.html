Fields of study

Three materials, one method

The library brings together research on the three principal materials of Chinese antique art. Each is studied through the same principle: authenticity is established not by a single feature, but by a coherent system of material, technological and stylistic evidence.

Porcelain

History, styles, marks and symbols; export, imperial and monochrome wares; criteria for distinguishing genuine antique porcelain from later copies and forgeries.

Explore porcelain →

Jade

Nephrite and its imitations, carving techniques and tool marks, surface alteration and patina as evidence of age.

Explore jade →

Bronze

Ritual bronze forms, casting technology, corrosion layers and patina, and the diagnostic value of trace elements.

Explore bronze →

Monographs

The foundation of the library: two reference monographs by Professor Nona Dmitrievna Dronova, published in Russian and presented here in English translation.

What You Need to Know About Antique Chinese Porcelain

Что надо знать о старинном китайском фарфоре
Author: Nona Dmitrievna Dronova
Bibliographic record

Dronova, Nona Dmitrievna. Что надо знать о старинном китайском фарфоре [What You Need to Know About Antique Chinese Porcelain]: reference encyclopaedia. Moscow: KEM Printing House LLC, 2016. 288 pp. ISBN 978-5-9908782-6-6.

Дронова Н. Д. Что надо знать о старинном китайском фарфоре: справочник-энциклопедия. — М.: ООО «Типография КЕМ», 2016. — 288 с. — ISBN 978-5-9908782-6-6.
UDC 621.315.612 · BBK 35.41+65.304.9

A reference encyclopaedia that provides the information needed to attribute antique Chinese porcelain in accordance with international standards. Separate chapters are devoted to the appraisal and diagnosis of export, imperial and monochrome porcelain; the book examines the development of porcelain styles and the marks found on antique Chinese porcelain, and gives practical recommendations for detecting forgeries.

What You Need to Know About Antique Chinese Jade

Что надо знать о старинном китайском нефрите
Author: Nona Dmitrievna Dronova
Bibliographic record

Dronova, Nona Dmitrievna. Что надо знать о старинном китайском нефрите [What You Need to Know About Antique Chinese Jade]: handbook in questions and answers. Moscow: KEM LLC, 2017. 196 pp. ISBN 978-5-9909531-3-0.

Дронова Н. Д. Что надо знать о старинном китайском нефрите: справочник в виде вопросов и ответов. — М.: ООО «КЕМ», 2017. — 196 с. — ISBN 978-5-9909531-3-0.
UDC 621.315.612 · BBK 35.41+65.304.9

A handbook in the form of questions and answers on the natural ageing of jade in burials and the diagnostic signs of its antiquity. Particular attention is given to the causes of the characteristic ring-shaped iron-rich formations crystallised on the surface of carved artefacts and to the zoning of external corrosion on ancient Chinese jades. A separate chapter examines the noble patinas of ancient bronzes.

Original Russian edition (PDF): attached to the Zenodo article doi:10.5281/zenodo.23446188

Diagnostic Atlas

Diagnostic Atlas of Chinese Porcelain

A scientific reference system of documented objects from the Tang to the Qing dynasty. Each entry records the image, the diagnostic feature, its description, the method of observation and the limits of its interpretation.

About the author

Professor Nona Dmitrievna Dronova

Doctor of Technical Sciences, Ph.D. in Geology and Mineralogy, gemmologist with more than forty years of practice; Chair of the Collegium of Expert Gemmologists of the Russian Federation and Scientific Director of the Global Diagnostic Atlas of Ancient Chinese Ceramics.

Author of non-destructive methods for the authentication of Chinese porcelain (BEINOS, DEE, LIE, PLIC), jade (STA) and bronze (TETA).
