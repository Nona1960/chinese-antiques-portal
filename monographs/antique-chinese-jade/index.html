Abstract

A handbook in the form of questions and answers on the natural ageing of jade in burials and the diagnostic signs of its antiquity. Particular attention is given to the causes of the characteristic ring-shaped iron-rich formations crystallised on the surface of carved artefacts and to the zoning of external corrosion on ancient Chinese jades. A separate chapter examines the noble patinas of ancient bronzes.
