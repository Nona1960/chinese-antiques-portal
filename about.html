About the Project

A scientific digital library on the attribution and authentication of Chinese antiques.

Purpose

Chinese Antiques — Scientific Library of Research and Attribution brings together, in English, the monographs, research papers and reference materials of Professor Nona Dronova on Chinese porcelain, jade and bronze.

The aim is to make the methods of expert attribution transparent and verifiable: to show not only the conclusions, but the evidence on which they rest and the limits of that evidence.

Structure

  • Library — the author's own works: monographs, articles and reference books.
  • Diagnostic Atlas — documented reference objects with their diagnostic features.
  • Scientific literature — the complete list of the author’s scientific articles, grouped by dynasty.
  • Zenodo publications — selected methodological papers and the Zenodo records of the monographs.

Language

The monographs were written and published in Russian. The English edition is prepared chapter by chapter; each chapter page gives the original Russian title. Terms of Chinese origin are given in pinyin, with Chinese characters where they help identification.

Information on this site is provided for scientific and educational purposes. It does not constitute an authentication or valuation of any particular object.
